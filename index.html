Select two matching symbols. A connection is valid if it follows horizontal and vertical lines through empty spaces with zero, one, or two bends.

Gold-striped chips are shielded. Clear a pair directly above, below, left, or right of a shielded chip to break its shield. One nearby match is enough; the freed chip can then be matched normally. Undo restores shields too.

Choose from Classic, Ring, Cross, Islands, Bridge, and Staircase layouts. All gaps and empty sockets inside the board carry wires. Orange-striped wall blocks cannot carry wires and cannot be removed. Choose a shape and press New board; your current game stays put until then.

The edge is a hard boundary: wires cannot go around the outside. Clear every pair to finish a board. The campaign introduces shapes, shields, and walls over 12 stages; each clear unlocks the next. Endless mode keeps the original random boards.
