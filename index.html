Two Chip’ums. One clear path.

Tap two matching Chip’ums. A connection is valid if it follows horizontal and vertical lines through empty spaces with zero, one, or two bends.

Gold-striped chips are shielded. Clear a pair directly above, below, left, or right of a shielded chip to break its shield. One nearby match is enough; the freed chip can then be matched normally. Double shields, with a thick gold rim and a 2 badge, need two nearby matches: the first cracks them, the second breaks them. Undo restores shields too.

On Bedtime levels (the moon) Chip’ums doze off over time. A sleepy Chip’um has to be your first tap: tap it to wake it, then tap its partner. Matches also wake sleepers right beside them. If everyone falls asleep, it’s lights out.

Choose from Classic, Ring, Cross, Islands, Bridge, and Staircase layouts. All gaps and empty sockets inside the board carry wires. Orange-striped wall blocks cannot carry wires and cannot be removed. Choose a shape and press New board; your current game stays put until then.

The edge is a hard boundary: wires cannot go around the outside. Clear every pair to finish a board. The campaign introduces shapes, shields, and walls over 19 stages; each clear unlocks the next. Endless mode keeps the original random boards.

Hint highlights a currently available pair. If your choices leave no pairs available, undo a move or restart the same board. Every starting layout is checked for a complete solution.
